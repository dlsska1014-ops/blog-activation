# Affiliate Guidelines

Use this reference for Coupang Partners TOP 5 posts.

## Product Selection

Choose products by combining:

- Seasonal demand.
- Search trend fit.
- Clear buyer intent.
- Practical usefulness.
- Likely order value.
- Availability and current price range.
- Review quality and return-risk signals when available.

Do not claim exact sales volume, ranking, or profitability unless verified from a reliable current source.

## Post Structure

1. Affiliate disclosure near the top.
2. Reader situation and buying criteria.
3. TOP 5 comparison table.
4. Individual product sections:
   - Who it fits.
   - Key strengths.
   - Watch-outs.
   - Buying tip.
5. Final recommendation by reader type.
6. FAQ.

## Required Disclosure

Include a clear Korean disclosure such as:

"이 포스팅은 쿠팡 파트너스 활동의 일환으로, 이에 따른 일정액의 수수료를 제공받습니다. 구매자에게 추가 비용이 발생하지 않습니다."

## Writing Rules

- Do not overpromise product performance.
- Do not invent hands-on experience.
- Use "추천 대상", "확인할 점", "비교 포인트" instead of absolute claims.
- Keep the tone practical and buyer-friendly.
