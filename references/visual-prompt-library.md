# Visual Prompt Library

Use this to generate or plan blog visuals. Keep images truthful and avoid fake official branding. Read `visual-quality-standard.md` first and use the `photorealistic-natural` image-generation class for editorial scenes.

Read `visual-asset-policy.md` first. Use AI generation mainly for custom thumbnails and generic explanatory illustrations. For body images, prefer official/source screenshots with notes, licensed images, user-provided images, or self-created cards/tables.

## Thumbnail: Event Benefit

Subject: realistic editorial scene of a Korean reader comparing travel dates, accommodation options, or benefit conditions on a phone and notebook; show the decision moment, not a generic discount card
Style: candid documentary editorial photograph, eye-level 35mm look, available natural light, ordinary paper and fabric texture, slightly imperfect arrangement
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Text: none during AI generation; add exact Korean later with a deterministic overlay
Avoid: fake official seal, copied brand logo, exaggerated discount claim, fake interface, random letters, malformed hands, perfect symmetry, glossy plastic texture, staged advertising pose

## Thumbnail: Product TOP 5

Subject: realistic product-category buying situation with three distinct use cases or a single decision scene, not exact copied product photos
Style: practical household editorial photography, natural mixed light, varied object wear and spacing, observed rather than staged
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Text: none during AI generation; add exact Korean later with a deterministic overlay
Avoid: exact product images unless provided or properly sourced, fake logos, impossible controls, duplicated objects, showroom perfection, floating products

## Thumbnail: Seasonal Household Problem

Subject: believable Korean home scene showing the actual problem and one useful action, such as damp laundry near a dehumidifier or summer travel items being checked before packing
Style: documentary editorial photo, physically coherent natural light, useful detail visible, subtle lived-in texture, no staged stock-photo gesture
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Text: none during AI generation
Avoid: extreme before/after split, medical claims, random labels, clutter that hides the problem, HDR glow, fake condensation, perfect symmetry

## Thumbnail: Family Outing Or Camping

Subject: wide contextual scene showing the facility, setup, route decision, or family-use condition without identifiable faces
Style: practical travel editorial, eye-level documentary camera, realistic weather and equipment proportions, diffuse light that matches the sky, ordinary wrinkles and mud or moisture where appropriate
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Text: none during AI generation
Avoid: copied venue photos, branded tents, unsafe child behavior, fantasy scenery presented as a real place, synthetic rain streaks, sunny shadows during heavy rain, duplicated equipment

## Body Image: Official Event Or Policy

Use source screenshot only when it clarifies dates, eligibility, steps, or terms.
Required notes: source name, URL, checked date, and what may change.
Avoid: cropping out context in a misleading way, fake official-looking edits, hiding conditions.

## Body Image: Licensed Or Safe Stock

Use only when the reuse terms are clear.
Required notes: source URL and license/reuse basis.
Avoid: random search-result images, copied blog thumbnails, watermarked images.

## Summary Card

Subject: checklist card summarizing key conditions
Style: restrained editorial note, small accent rule, neutral background, modest heading, no top or footer bar
Aspect ratio: 4:3 or 16:9
Text: 3 to 5 short bullet labels
Avoid: tiny text, overloaded details, giant headings, rounded presentation panels, repeated template colors

## Comparison Table Image

Subject: visual comparison table for buyer criteria
Style: understated editorial grid, readable Korean labels, thin rules, no dark title bar
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Text: price band, best for, watch-out
Avoid: too many columns, slide-deck styling, decorative icons

## FAQ Card

Subject: simple Q&A card for common reader questions
Style: calm, readable, lightly branded
Aspect ratio: 4:3
Text: 2 to 3 short questions
Avoid: long answers inside image

## Prompt Suffix

Append when appropriate:

"No text, no logos, no watermark, no fake user interface, no duplicate objects, realistic proportions, physically coherent light and weather, natural surface texture, candid editorial composition, mobile-safe crop."
