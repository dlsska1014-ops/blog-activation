# Reader Intent Map

Use this to match content structure to why the reader is searching.

## Intent Types

### Know

The reader wants to understand a topic.

Use:

- Explainer.
- Key facts table.
- FAQ.
- Source notes.

Avoid heavy affiliate links.

### Do

The reader wants to apply, check, register, compare, or avoid a mistake.

Use:

- Step-by-step checklist.
- Condition table.
- Mistake-prevention section.
- Deadline and source notes.

### Buy

The reader is close to purchasing.

Use:

- Criteria first.
- Budget bands.
- TOP 5 comparison.
- Who should buy / who should skip.
- Affiliate disclosure if links are used.

### Compare

The reader has options and needs a decision.

Use:

- Side-by-side table.
- Reader-type recommendations.
- Trade-offs.
- Watch-outs.

### Update

The reader wants what changed recently.

Use:

- Checked date.
- Before/after.
- What changed.
- What to do now.

## Intent Fit Rule

If the post structure does not match the reader intent, rewrite the outline before drafting.
