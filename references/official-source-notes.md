# Official Source Notes

Use this file as a compact reminder of verified policy-oriented sources. Re-check sources when rules may have changed.

## Naver Blog Search Exposure

Naver Blog help explains search result order as a combined relevance judgment using multiple factors such as whether keywords are included, freshness, content quality, and popularity. It also explains that people may call ranking drops "low quality," but that does not mean there is a simple public grade to optimize around.

Operational takeaway:

- Do not chase "low-quality avoidance hacks."
- Improve originality, relevance, source quality, topic consistency, and reader usefulness.
- Track exposure and performance instead of assuming one cause.

## FTC Economic Relationship Disclosure

Korea Fair Trade Commission materials emphasize clear disclosure of economic relationships in recommendations and endorsements, including cases where compensation or employment relationships can affect credibility.

Operational takeaway:

- Put affiliate disclosure near the recommendation, preferably near the top.
- Do not hide disclosure only at the bottom.
- Keep product claims supportable and avoid misleading urgency.
