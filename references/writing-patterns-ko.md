# Korean Writing Patterns

Use these synthetic Korean writing patterns to make posts more natural while staying original. Do not copy real influencer wording.

## Preferred First-Screen Pattern

1. One situational sentence.
2. One reason the topic matters now.
3. One promise of what the post will clarify.
4. One caution if there is a benefit, product, or deadline.

Example:

"행사 혜택이 크게 보이면 일단 좋아 보이지만, 실제로는 조건을 놓치면 체감 할인이 줄어드는 경우가 많습니다. 오늘은 구매 전에 먼저 확인할 부분과 예산별로 볼 만한 조합을 나눠서 정리해볼게요."

## Natural Title Styles

- Benefit + condition: "온누리 20% 페이백, 구매 전 조건부터 확인하세요"
- Situation + solution: "장마철 제습기 고르기 전, 방 크기부터 보세요"
- Audience + choice: "부모님 선물용 마사지기, 이런 기준이면 고르기 쉽습니다"
- Mistake prevention: "행사 상품 살 때 놓치기 쉬운 제한 5가지"
- Goal + reality check: "애드센스 월 100만 원, 2026년에 현실적으로 보려면 3가지만 확인하세요"

## Paragraph Rhythm

- One idea per paragraph.
- Use short paragraphs for mobile reading.
- Put warnings or exceptions on their own line.
- Use tables for comparison, not decoration.

## Human-Like Editorial Moves

- Start from a real reader situation.
- Admit uncertainty when source details can change.
- Say who should skip a product or event.
- Explain the order of decision-making.
- Use "정리하면" only near actual summary points.
- Use truthful process language when the work was performed: "공식 안내를 다시 확인해보니", "조건을 표로 나눠보면".
- Add two concrete reader scenarios to long posts.
- Put a mini-verdict before a long explanation when the reader needs a decision.
- Let headings carry advice or contrast instead of using only category labels.
- For revenue, SEO, policy, and side-income explainers, start by lowering the guarantee claim, then give the conditions that make the goal realistic.

## Anti-Uniformity

- Do not give every section the same number of paragraphs.
- Do not end three consecutive paragraphs with the same verb or tone.
- Do not repeat "확인하세요" as the default instruction.
- Mix one-line verdicts, medium explanations, a compact list, and a useful visual.
- Keep cautions next to the relevant recommendation rather than collecting all cautions at the end.

## Avoid

- "오늘은 ~에 대해 알아보겠습니다" as the default opening.
- Repeating the same ending in every sentence.
- Overusing "필수", "무조건", "역대급", "대박".
- Writing product recommendations without selection criteria.
- Pretending to personally test or purchase something.

## Closing Pattern

End with a practical next action:

- "구매 전에는 행사 기간과 대상 제품을 먼저 확인하세요."
- "예산이 정해져 있다면 기능보다 사용 환경부터 좁히는 편이 안전합니다."
- "조건이 바뀔 수 있으니 결제 직전 공식 안내를 한 번 더 확인하는 게 좋습니다."
