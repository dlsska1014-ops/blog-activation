## Weekly Mix

Aim for:

- 2 to 3 information/event posts.
- 2 seasonal/search posts.
- 1 to 2 affiliate TOP 5 posts.
- 1 update or FAQ post if performance data suggests it.

Do not force three posts every day if topic quality is weak.

## Daily Selection

Before choosing topics, ask:

- Is this timely this week?
- Is there a distinct reader intent?
- Is the topic already covered recently?
- Does it fit the blog's positioning?
- Can it be supported by current sources?

## Calendar Rule

Plan seasonal posts 2 to 4 weeks before peak demand. Use event posts closer to the confirmed date and only after checking current conditions.
