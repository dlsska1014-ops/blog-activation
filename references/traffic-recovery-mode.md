# Traffic Recovery Mode

Use this when recent traffic declines, new posts receive little search inflow, or publication defects damage trust.

## Trigger

Enter recovery mode when any condition is true:

- The latest complete 7-day average is at least 25% below the previous comparable 7-day average.
- Multiple new posts remain below 5 views after 7 days.
- Duplicate posts, raw Markdown, broken images, or internal notes are publicly visible.
- Affiliate-first posts outnumber trust-building posts in the recent week.

## Publishing Policy

- Continue planning and drafting three candidates, but publicly publish at most one fully verified Naver post per day.
- Stagger the other drafts or keep them as drafts; do not publish simply to meet a count.
- Limit affiliate-first posts to two per week until two consecutive weekly windows stabilize or improve.
- Repair the highest-risk public defects before adding more similar content.
- Do not make multiple small edits to the same post in one day.

## Recovery Content Mix

Use evidence from the blog's own statistics:

- 50% proven cluster: specific family outing, camping, place, facility, product model, or real-use query.
- 30% adjacent problem-solving: seasonal preparation, mistakes, fees, facilities, parking, weather, setup, maintenance, or checklist content tied to the proven cluster.
- 20% monetization: buyer-intent content adjacent to the proven cluster, with verified products and links.

Prefer long-tail questions already appearing in search inflow. Examples include a place plus facility, a model plus review, a product type plus technical criterion, or an event plus failure condition.

## Original-Evidence Gate

Every recovery post must contain at least one:

- User-owned photo or confirmed first-hand observation.
- Current official fact plus an original checklist or calculation.
- Product specification comparison using verified source data.
- Location-specific detail such as parking, facilities, route, opening conditions, or family suitability.
- Follow-up answer to an actual inflow keyword or reader question.

Reject a broad topic when it can only restate information already common across many posts.

## Exit

Leave recovery mode only after:

- Two consecutive complete 7-day windows are stable or improving.
- No unresolved public formatting or image defects remain in the recovery set.
- At least two new posts receive search inflow from their intended long-tail questions.

Do not promise a ranking or traffic result. Recovery mode reduces avoidable risk and concentrates evidence; it cannot guarantee exposure.
