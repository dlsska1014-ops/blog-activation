# Simulation Cases

Use these synthetic cases to test whether the skill selects topics, structures drafts, and handles affiliate posts correctly. They are examples for reasoning, not claims about current trends.

## Case 1: Electronics Payback Event

Scenario:

- A major electronics brand runs a limited payback event.
- Readers want to know what to buy, whether the benefit is real, and what conditions apply.

Expected output:

- One event/benefit explainer.
- Budget-based purchase scenarios.
- A conditions/exclusions table.
- Image notes for event summary and benefit calculation.
- No unsupported claim about actual sales ranking.

## Case 2: Rainy Season Home Goods

Scenario:

- Rainy season starts and humidity-related products are searched more often.

Expected output:

- Seasonal search post about dehumidifier or shoe dryer selection.
- Coupang Partners TOP 5 can focus on a specific product cluster.
- Include room size, noise, maintenance, and electricity cost watch-outs.

## Case 3: Parent Gift Season

Scenario:

- Gift demand rises before a family holiday.

Expected output:

- Gift guide by budget and recipient situation.
- Avoid medical promises.
- Include "who should skip" for risky product categories.
- Use thumbnail text under 16 Korean characters.

## Case 4: Public Benefit Or Local Currency

Scenario:

- A local or public benefit changes conditions.

Expected output:

- Information/news explainer.
- Checked date and official source notes.
- Eligibility, application method, deadline, and exceptions.
- No affiliate angle unless naturally related and useful.

## Case 5: Back-To-School Tech

Scenario:

- Students and parents compare tablets, laptops, printers, and accessories.

Expected output:

- Seasonal search post or Coupang Partners TOP 5.
- Product categories should be separated by use case.
- Include budget bands and watch-outs.

## Case 6: Viral Keyword With Weak Buyer Intent

Scenario:

- A celebrity or viral phrase creates search volume but has weak relevance to the blog.

Expected output:

- Reject or use only if it can become a helpful consumer explainer.
- Do not chase traffic that damages blog positioning.

## Case 7: High-Ticket Product With Risk

Scenario:

- Expensive appliance category has high order value but many specs and return concerns.

Expected output:

- Use comparison-heavy structure.
- Include installation, size, after-service, and return-risk notes.
- Avoid pushing a single product as universally best.

## Case 8: Fast-Moving Discount

Scenario:

- A short discount window may expire quickly.

Expected output:

- Require browsing and checked date.
- Add "prices and stock can change" note.
- Prefer draft only if still timely.
