# Affiliate Link Density

Use this to keep the blog trust-first while still monetizing.

## Core Rule

Affiliate links should support a useful recommendation, not become the reason the post exists.

## Post-Level Guidance

- Information/news posts: usually 0 affiliate links.
- Event/benefit posts: 0 to 2 links only if directly useful.
- Buying guides: 3 to 5 links if each product has a distinct use case.
- Coupang TOP 5 posts: 5 primary links are acceptable, plus avoid excessive repeated buttons.

## Blog-Level Mix

Across a week, avoid making most posts affiliate-first. A healthy mix is:

- More helpful information posts than affiliate posts.
- Affiliate posts tied to clear buyer intent.
- No clusters where every support post is product-heavy.
- No more than two affiliate-first publications in a rolling seven-day window.
- No affiliate-first publications on consecutive days.
- In autonomous mode, choose information content when the recent receipt history is missing or ambiguous.

## Disclosure

Place the Coupang Partners disclosure near the top before recommendations. Use clear language and do not hide it in small text or only at the bottom.

## Red Flags

- Multiple links to nearly identical products.
- Same product pushed across unrelated posts.
- Affiliate link before the reader understands the selection criteria.
- No "who should skip" or watch-out section.
- Product claims stronger than the source supports.
