# Research Sources

Use current sources whenever dates, prices, rankings, events, products, benefits, or news are involved.

## Daily Source Sweep

Check sources in this order:

1. Naver search results and Naver Blog results for the target keyword.
2. Naver Shopping or major shopping pages for product categories and price bands.
3. Official event or brand pages for dates, conditions, exclusions, and benefit details.
4. News sources for issue background and current developments.
5. Tistory or Google results for long-form structure ideas.
6. Coupang product/category pages when preparing Coupang Partners posts.

## What To Extract

Extract facts:

- Dates, eligibility, prices, stock or availability signals, conditions, exclusions.
- Product specs and comparison points.
- Current reader questions and repeated confusion points.
- Source name and checked date.

Extract patterns:

- Title formulas.
- Opening hooks.
- Section order.
- Table or image placement.
- CTA or closing style.

Do not extract:

- Full paragraphs from other creators.
- Unique personal stories.
- Private data.
- Unsupported claims about sales volume or income.

## Source Note Format

Use this format in draft notes:

- Source:
- Checked date:
- Key facts:
- Uncertainties:
- How it affects the article:

## Trend Scoring

Score topic candidates from 1 to 5:

- Timeliness: Is this relevant today or this week?
- Reader pain: Does it solve a concrete question?
- Search demand: Are people likely searching it?
- Monetization fit: Can it lead to a useful product or affiliate angle?
- Trust fit: Can the post provide value without exaggeration?

Prioritize topics with a total score of 18 or higher.
