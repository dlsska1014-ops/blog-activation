- Review window: daily views from 2026-06-12 through 2026-06-26; ranking window 2026-06-15 through 2026-06-21.
- Views: latest complete 7-day average about 16.9; preceding comparable 7-day average about 26.6; decline about 36.6%.
- Strong posts: `평택 '내리문화공원' 나들이` 74 weekly views; `첫 텐트 피칭 '카즈미T-250 '후기` 26; `충주 '어게인 캠핑장'후기` 11.

- Weakness: broad topic spread, generic information posts, frequent affiliate formats, duplicate/raw-Markdown posts, and corrupted visual text.
- Lesson: prioritize original local/camping evidence and long-tail reader questions; enter traffic recovery mode.
- Next action: three candidates per day, at most one verified public post; affiliate-first maximum two per week until recovery exit criteria are met.

## Weekly Review

Every 7 daily runs, review:

- Best 3 titles.
- Best 3 topics.
- Weakest 3 posts.
- Keywords that appeared repeatedly.
- Product categories with high buyer intent.
- Layout patterns that improved readability.
- Updates to make in `style-memory.md`, `voice-guide.md`, or `post-templates.md`.

## Decision Rules

- Repeat a topic cluster if it produces search inflow or comments.
- Rewrite title patterns that get low views despite timely topics.
- Expand product categories that produce affiliate clicks.
- Avoid product categories with high curiosity but low purchase intent unless they support trust-building.
- Preserve reader trust over short-term clickbait.
