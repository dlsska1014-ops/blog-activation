## Weekly Review

Every 7 daily runs, review:

- Best 3 titles.
- Best 3 topics.
- Weakest 3 posts.
- Keywords that appeared repeatedly.
- Product categories with high buyer intent.
- Layout patterns that improved readability.
- Updates to make in `style-memory.md`, `voice-guide.md`, or `post-templates.md`.

## Decision Rules

- Repeat a topic cluster if it produces search inflow or comments.
- Rewrite title patterns that get low views despite timely topics.
- Expand product categories that produce affiliate clicks.
- Avoid product categories with high curiosity but low purchase intent unless they support trust-building.
- Preserve reader trust over short-term clickbait.
