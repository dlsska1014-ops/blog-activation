# AdSense SEO Growth Playbook

Use this when a post, topic board, or strategy note discusses AdSense, AdFit, AdPost, SEO income, blog monetization, traffic growth, or "monthly income" goals.

This file absorbs durable lessons from user-provided source text about 2026 AdSense strategy, but do not treat that source as verified policy. Convert it into source-checked, reader-first operating rules.

## Core Position

Frame AdSense income as conditional, not promised.

Use:

- "가능성은 있지만 조건이 필요합니다."
- "트래픽보다 검색 의도와 신뢰가 먼저입니다."
- "수익은 주제, 유입 품질, 광고 단가, 배치, 정책 준수에 따라 달라집니다."

Avoid:

- guaranteed monthly income,
- exact income timelines without user data,
- unsupported CPC, CPM, CTR, or revenue averages,
- screenshots or stories that imply private earnings proof when the user did not provide them.

## Three-Stage Roadmap

For AdSense/SEO income explainers, build the article around these three stages.

### 1. Trust And Content Specialization

The post should explain why a blog earns trust before it earns stable ad income.

Include:

- one clear niche or reader problem,
- search-intent fit,
- source-backed facts,
- realistic cautions,
- original comparison, checklist, table, or decision criteria,
- experience only when the user supplied real notes or owned evidence.

High-value categories such as finance, insurance, health, IT devices, education, and legal-adjacent topics can attract strong intent, but they also carry higher trust and source requirements. Treat finance, health, legal, insurance, investing, medical, and safety topics as YMYL-sensitive.

### 2. SEO, UX, And Retention

The post should connect search exposure to usefulness, not keyword stuffing.

Require:

- one primary reader question,
- a first-screen answer or mini-verdict,
- mobile-readable short paragraphs,
- helpful headings,
- at least one comparison table, checklist, FAQ, or scenario block when the topic is complex,
- source dates for current claims,
- image roles that help understanding rather than generic text cards,
- internal link plan when a related owned post exists.

For Tistory/SEO adaptations, include a meta-style summary, structured headings, image alt text intent, and a source note when facts are current.

### 3. Revenue Diversification

Explain revenue as lanes, not a single jackpot.

Possible lanes:

- Naver AdPost for domestic traffic baseline,
- Tistory AdSense or AdFit for searchable evergreen content,
- Coupang Partners for buyer-intent posts with required disclosure,
- sponsored/review work only after trust and audience fit are proven,
- SNS, short-form, newsletter, or simple productized resources only when they reuse a proven post cluster.

Do not force every information post into monetization language. In non-affiliate public posts, avoid words such as "제휴", "파트너스", "광고 수익 극대화" unless the article is explicitly about monetization strategy.

## Policy And Source Check Gate

Before drafting or updating any AdSense/SEO policy claim, verify current official sources.

Required checks:

- Google Search guidance for AI-assisted content and helpful content.
- Google spam policies when discussing automated or scaled content.
- AdSense program policies for clicks, ad placement, invalid traffic, and ad/content balance.
- Consent/CMP/TCF requirements only when the audience or site operation involves EEA, UK, Switzerland, or global traffic.

Do not import unverified claims from source text, including:

- "2026년 2월에 업데이트된 Helpful Content 가이드라인" unless directly verified,
- "2026년 3월 1일 정책 마감일" unless directly verified,
- "수익 50~70% 감소" unless a reliable current source supports the exact range,
- "한국 평균 CPC 0.1~0.2달러" unless current data source is cited.

If a policy claim cannot be verified, either remove it or write it as a general caution without numbers or deadline.

## AI-Assisted Content Rule

Do not write for AI-detector evasion. Write for usefulness, originality, and truthful editorial presence.

Allowed:

- using AI to structure research,
- turning official facts into readable explanations,
- adding user-provided experience,
- adding original comparison and decision support.

Blocked:

- mass-producing similar pages for search manipulation,
- rewriting existing posts with little added value,
- inventing personal experience,
- adding fake typos, fake memories, or staged first-person claims,
- copying a creator's recognizable flow or phrasing.

## YMYL-Sensitive Handling

For finance, insurance, health, legal, investing, public support, and safety topics:

- use official or primary sources first,
- avoid diagnosis, prescription, investment instruction, legal advice, or guaranteed savings,
- state that the post is general information when needed,
- include "누가 조심해야 하는지" or "확인해야 할 조건",
- prefer checklists and source links over strong recommendations.

## Source Text Cleanup Gate

When user-provided source text is used as learning material, clean it before applying.

Remove or rewrite:

- duplicate paragraphs,
- repeated introductions,
- pasted category labels such as "광고 및 마케팅", "검색엔진", "블로깅 리소스 및 서비스" when they are not part of the article,
- FAQ numbering that appears in the wrong section,
- private operation notes,
- unsupported numbers and dates,
- expressions that sound like generic AI summaries.

Public drafts must not expose learning notes or say "이 글에서는..." repeatedly as a mechanical transition.

## Article Pattern

Use this structure for an AdSense/SEO income post:

1. Reality-check opening: lower the guarantee claim and name the condition.
2. First-screen checklist: traffic quality, topic trust, search intent, policy safety, user value.
3. Stage 1: trust and specialized content.
4. Stage 2: SEO, mobile readability, image/UX, and retention.
5. Stage 3: monetization lanes and data loop.
6. Cautions: policy, YMYL, fake experience, unsupported income numbers.
7. Practical next action: what to check or improve this week.

## Metadata

For every AdSense/SEO strategy post, record:

- `primary_reader_question`
- `income_claim_level`: `none`, `conditional`, or `source_verified`
- `policy_sources_checked`
- `ymyl_sensitive`: true or false
- `unsupported_number_removed`: true or false
- `source_text_cleanup_confirmed`: true or false
- `next_metric_to_review`: views, search queries, CTR, RPM, clicks, conversions, or retention proxy
