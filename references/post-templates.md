# Post Templates

Use the matching template for the selected topic. Adapt structure naturally instead of forcing every section into every post.

## Information Or News Explainer

Use for current issues, policies, consumer news, platform changes, and event announcements.

Structure:

1. Title options.
2. First 5 lines:
   - What happened?
   - Who should care?
   - What should the reader check first?
3. Quick summary.
4. Key facts table:
   - Date
   - Target
   - Benefit or change
   - Conditions
   - Source checked date
5. Why it matters.
6. Step-by-step action or decision guide.
7. Common misunderstandings.
8. FAQ.
9. Closing next step.

Naver image rhythm:

- Image 1 after opening: event/news summary image or official screenshot note.
- Image 2 near key facts: table or condition summary.
- Image 3 near action guide: checklist or step image.

## Event And Benefit Shopping Guide

Use for payback events, brand promotions, card benefits, local currency benefits, and limited-time discounts.

Structure:

1. Benefit-led title.
2. "이 행사는 누가 보면 좋은가" opening.
3. Event summary table.
4. Best purchase scenarios:
   - Low budget
   - Mid budget
   - High budget
5. Recommended product/category list.
6. Benefit calculation examples.
7. Conditions and exclusions.
8. Mistakes to avoid.
9. FAQ.
10. Final recommendation by reader type.

## Seasonal Search Post

Use for seasonal products, travel, gifting, weather, moving, school, holidays, and annual routines.

Structure:

1. Seasonal problem opening.
2. Quick recommendation by situation.
3. Selection criteria.
4. Recommended options or checklist.
5. Price/budget guide.
6. Timing guide: when to buy, prepare, reserve, or compare.
7. FAQ.
8. Tags with seasonal keywords.

## Coupang Partners TOP 5

Use for the daily affiliate post.

Before writing, score the product category and candidates with `affiliate-scoring.md`.

Structure:

1. Required affiliate disclosure near the top.
2. Buyer situation opening.
3. Selection criteria:
   - Price band
   - Review/quality signal
   - Seasonal fit
   - Practical use case
   - Return-risk or caution point
4. TOP 5 comparison table.
5. Product sections:
   - Recommended for
   - Strengths
   - Watch-outs
   - Buying tip
6. Final pick by reader type.
7. FAQ.

Do not say a product is "best-selling" or "highest sales" unless verified from a current source.

## Tistory Adaptation

After Naver draft is complete:

- Add meta description.
- Convert soft Naver headings into clear H2/H3 headings.
- Add a table of contents for long posts.
- Keep source notes visible.
- Reduce decorative spacing.
