# Title A/B Testing

Use this to generate and score multiple title candidates before draft-save.

## Generate Title Types

Create 5 to 10 title options:

- Search-friendly title.
- Benefit-led title.
- Mistake-prevention title.
- Audience-specific title.
- Timeliness title.
- Comparison title.

## Score Each Title

Score from 1 to 5:

- Accuracy: Does the body support the title?
- Search fit: Does it include natural keywords?
- Click clarity: Does the reader know why to click?
- Trust: Does it avoid exaggeration?
- Distinction: Is it different from recent titles?

Use the title with the best total score, not the loudest title.

## Avoid

- Clickbait that the body cannot satisfy.
- Repeated title formulas.
- Too many punctuation marks.
- Unsupported words such as "최저가", "역대급", "1위", "완벽".
