# Duplicate Intent Check

Use this before drafting to avoid low-value repetition.

## Compare Against Recent Posts

Check the planned draft against the last 14 to 30 days of posts when available.

Compare:

- Main keyword.
- Reader question.
- Product category.
- Event or benefit.
- Title structure.
- Opening angle.
- Table/checklist topic.
- Affiliate product set.

## Risk Levels

Low risk:

- Same cluster, different reader intent.
- New facts, new date, or changed conditions.
- Different budget, audience, or use case.

Medium risk:

- Similar keyword but different angle.
- Same product category with a new seasonal reason.
- Same event but a deeper FAQ or mistake-focused post.

High risk:

- Same reader question.
- Same product list with reordered rankings.
- Same title pattern and same conclusion.
- Only minor wording changes.

## Decision

- Low risk: draft normally.
- Medium risk: make the angle visibly different and add new source value.
- High risk: update the old post, merge the idea into a cluster, or reject the draft.
