# Voice Guide

Use this file to keep posts natural and consistent. The goal is readable Korean blog writing with a human editorial feel, not evasion of AI detectors.

## Tone

- Warm, practical, and observant.
- Write like a person who checked the details and is organizing them for a busy reader.
- Use light conversational phrases, but keep the information density high.
- Prefer "이런 분들은 먼저 확인해보세요" over forced excitement.

## Sentence Rhythm

- Mix short sentences with medium explanatory sentences.
- Put important cautions on their own line.
- Use 1 to 3 line paragraphs for Naver Blog.
- Avoid long chains of similar endings such as "-습니다" repeated too many times.

## Useful Korean Bridges

Use sparingly:

- "여기서 중요한 건"
- "생각보다 헷갈리는 부분은"
- "정리하면"
- "구매 전에 볼 부분은"
- "이런 분들에게는 괜찮고"
- "반대로 이런 경우라면"
- "놓치기 쉬운 조건은"

## Emoticons And Emoji

- Use only when the post mood allows it.
- Prefer 0 to 3 per post by default.
- Do not place emoji in every heading.
- Do not use emoji to replace structure.
- Use simple markers such as `✔`, `※`, or `Q.` only when they improve scanning.

## Human Detail

Add grounded details:

- "예산이 30만 원대라면"
- "부모님 선물용이면"
- "자취방처럼 공간이 좁다면"
- "행사 조건을 놓치면 체감 혜택이 줄어듭니다"

Do not invent:

- Personal purchase history.
- Direct product testing.
- Exact sales data.
- Private influencer metrics.

## Rewrite Pass

Before finalizing a draft:

1. Remove generic AI-like openings.
2. Replace vague claims with specific reader situations.
3. Add one caution or exception.
4. Vary paragraph length.
5. Make the first 5 lines answer why the post matters today.
