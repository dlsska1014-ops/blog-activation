## 2026-06-27 Latest Cross-Platform Review

Date: 2026-06-27
Run purpose: overall skill improvement after visual and editorial quality changes
Target topics: rainy-season camping, seasonal household problems, family water play, camping products, and event visits

### Naver Blog Observations

1. Source type: Naver Blog/search-surfaced current posts
   - Topic family: family outings and seasonal camping
   - Observed pattern: stronger posts narrow the broad season into a specific reader and task, such as a child facility, pet indoor activity, parking, changed operating condition, or one safety product.
   - Why it may work: the reader can recognize the exact situation and the post has a reason to exist beyond a generic forecast summary.
   - Reusable version: combine place or product identity with one current condition and two practical questions.
   - Risk to avoid: do not add a narrow personal angle without authentic notes or photos.
   - Experience basis: user-owned or creator-owned firsthand evidence.
   - Negative lesson: broad "total summary" titles often repeat the same uncertain dates and definitions.

2. Source type: Naver Blog/current photo-backed posts
   - Topic family: place visits, family facilities, and sponsored camping products
   - Observed pattern: original scene, facility, price-board, feature, and use photos are placed in the same order as the visit or product decision.
   - Why it may work: images prove details that text alone cannot establish and give readers a realistic preview.
   - Reusable version: prefer owned hero evidence, then context, practical detail, and decision photos; add a card only where comparison is clearer than a photo.
   - Risk to avoid: raw image count is not a quality signal, and near-duplicate frames add no value.
   - Experience basis: firsthand or disclosed sponsored use.
   - Negative lesson: merchant images cannot support invented long-term use claims.

3. Source type: Naver Blog/current low-trust examples
   - Topic family: seasonal forecasts
   - Observed pattern: duplicated titles, exposed generation labels, multilingual keyword blocks, and exact-looking dates appear in some posts.
   - Why it may work: it may increase keyword coverage, but it weakens trust and reader flow.
   - Reusable version: none; keep one clean title and label forecasts as reference values.
   - Risk to avoid: do not learn visible automation artifacts or unsupported certainty.
   - Experience basis: research-only.
   - Negative lesson: search visibility alone does not make a post a learning-quality example.

### Tistory Observations

1. Source type: Tistory/search-structured current examples
   - Topic family: rainy-season household and safety guidance
   - Observed pattern: a one-sentence thesis appears before the table, followed by a short editorial judgment explaining what matters most.
   - Why it may work: the reader receives a decision rule rather than an encyclopedia summary.
   - Reusable version: put one evidence-backed verdict before the detail and repeat the limitation near the table.
   - Risk to avoid: do not turn every section into a repetitive branded judgment block.
   - Experience basis: research-only.
   - Negative lesson: long structure without a distinct decision still feels generated.

2. Source type: Tistory/event and family-use examples
   - Topic family: camping events and family water play
   - Observed pattern: entry, route, budget, facility, preparation, and failure-prevention sections follow the reader's actual day.
   - Why it may work: chronological decision order makes a long search article easier to use.
   - Reusable version: structure practical posts by before-arrival, arrival, use, purchase, and exit decisions.
   - Risk to avoid: old schedules and prices need current official verification even when firsthand experience is genuine.
   - Experience basis: mixed official facts and firsthand notes.
   - Negative lesson: firsthand tone does not make stale operating information current.

### Candidate Style-Memory Updates

- Pattern: authentic experience basis plus original visual evidence should outrank generic trend fit in topic selection.
- Evidence count: repeated across 8 Naver examples, 5 Tistory examples, and the user's own performance history.
- Platform fit: both, with photo rhythm emphasized on Naver and decision structure emphasized on Tistory.
- Use in next draft: yes.
- Decision: update style-memory and publication manifest rules.

- Pattern: place one clear editorial verdict before detailed tables or long explanations.
- Evidence count: repeated across multiple Naver and Tistory examples.
- Platform fit: both.
- Use in next draft: yes.
