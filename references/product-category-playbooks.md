# Product Category Playbooks

Use this for recurring Coupang Partners and shopping guide categories. Re-check current prices, availability, and reviews before drafting.

## Dehumidifiers

Good for: rainy season, small homes, laundry drying.

Compare:

- Room size.
- Tank capacity.
- Noise.
- Electricity use.
- Drainage method.
- Maintenance.

Watch-outs:

- Oversized products for small rooms.
- Noise-sensitive bedrooms.
- Price changes during rainy season.

## Fans And Air Circulators

Good for: summer prep, energy-saving tips, small rooms.

Compare:

- Airflow distance.
- Noise.
- Cleaning ease.
- Oscillation.
- Remote/control options.

## Tablets And Laptops

Good for: back-to-school, work-from-home, parent/student guides.

Compare:

- Usage: study, video, office work, design.
- Storage.
- Weight.
- Battery.
- Accessories.

Watch-outs:

- Overbuying specs.
- Warranty and after-service.

## Parent Gifts

Good for: family month, holidays, birthdays.

Compare:

- Ease of use.
- Safety.
- Size/weight.
- Maintenance.
- Return/exchange risk.

Watch-outs:

- Medical-like claims.
- Products that require difficult setup.

## Storage And Moving Goods

Good for: moving season, spring cleaning, year-end organization.

Compare:

- Space size.
- Durability.
- Visibility.
- Stackability.
- Cleaning ease.
