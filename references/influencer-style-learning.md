# Influencer Style Learning

Use this guide when observing high-performing Naver Blog or Tistory posts. Learn reusable editorial patterns, not copied text.

## What To Observe

Observe:

- Title structure.
- First-screen hook.
- Paragraph length.
- Blank line rhythm.
- Image placement.
- Table or checklist use.
- How cautions are phrased.
- How the post transitions into recommendations.
- Comment-inducing closing questions.
- The first visual's role: scene, proof, comparison, or card.
- Whether visual captions add context rather than repeat headings.
- Truthful editorial moves such as source checking, prioritization, and balanced judgment.

Do not copy:

- Exact sentences.
- Unique jokes or personal stories.
- Creator-specific catchphrases.
- Image layouts that clearly identify a creator.
- Product claims without verification.

## Pattern Note Format

Record only abstracted lessons:

- Topic:
- Observed pattern:
- Why it likely works:
- Reusable version:
- Risk to avoid:
- Visual role worth testing:
- Trust device worth testing:

Example:

- Topic: seasonal home appliance guide
- Observed pattern: starts from room size and usage situation before listing products
- Why it likely works: readers can self-select before seeing recommendations
- Reusable version: open with buyer situation, then compare by space/use case
- Risk to avoid: pretending to personally test products

## Daily Learning Rule

Before observing current examples, read `daily-influencer-scan.md`. After observing examples, write abstract notes to `influencer-scan-log.md`.

After observing posts, update `style-memory.md` only if:

- The pattern appears across multiple posts or matches real performance.
- The lesson can be reused without copying.
- It improves reader clarity, trust, or scanning.

## Style Adaptation

Turn observed style into your own:

- Replace copied phrasing with neutral, practical Korean.
- Use the user's blog positioning from `blog-profile.md`.
- Keep one consistent editorial voice.
- Vary layout patterns across posts to avoid sameness.
- Use `editorial-presence-guide.md` to turn observations into an original reader scene, editorial priority, visual proof, and practical next action.
- Treat influencer-like quality as presence and usefulness, not excitement, decorative emoji, or first-person claims.
