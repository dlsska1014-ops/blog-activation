# Evidence And Experience Policy

Use this before topic selection and again before publication. The goal is to choose the strongest available evidence and never manufacture a visit, purchase, test, or personal result.

## Evidence Ladder

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. User-owned current photos plus user-provided visit or use notes.
2. User-provided experience notes without photos, clearly separated from current official facts.
3. Current official sources plus original comparison, interpretation, or decision support.
4. Licensed or merchant-approved images plus a research-only buying guide.
5. AI-generated scene images only as visual context, never as proof that a visit or product test happened.

Do not lower the evidence standard merely to keep a three-post schedule.

## Experience Basis

Record one value in every publish manifest:

- `research_only`: no visit, purchase, or use experience was supplied.
- `user_provided_experience`: the user supplied relevant notes that may be paraphrased.
- `user_owned_photo_report`: the user supplied reusable photos and enough context to describe them accurately.
- `sponsored_product_review`: a product was provided and the disclosure, use scope, and photo basis are documented.

For any basis other than `research_only`, add a concise `experience_note` such as "user confirmed a visit in June 2026 and supplied reusable photos." Do not store private schedules, addresses, account data, or sensitive family information.

## Research-Only Language

Allowed:

- "공식 안내를 다시 확인해보니"
- "현재 공개된 조건을 비교하면"
- "방문 전에는 주차와 운영시간을 먼저 보는 편이 안전합니다"

Blocked unless the user supplied the experience:

- "직접 다녀와 보니"
- "제가 써보니"
- "우리 아이가 좋아했어요"
- "작년에 구매했는데"
- "며칠 사용해본 결과"

Hypothetical reader scenarios are allowed when clearly framed with "라면", "경우", or "예정이라면".

## Experience-Backed Post

When reusable user evidence exists, prioritize at least one experience-backed candidate in the daily topic board. Build it around exact reader questions:

- parking and entry route,
- current operating condition,
- age or family suitability,
- facility, water depth, shade, restroom, food, or waiting condition,
- setup, storage, durability, or model-specific use,
- what changed since the previous visit or version.

Use original photos as evidence in the same order as the reader's visit or decision. Do not force a fixed photo count; keep only photos that answer a question or prove a condition.

## Sponsored And Product Content

For a provided product:

- Put the sponsorship disclosure before the review content.
- Separate supplied specifications from observed use.
- State the test scope and duration when the user provides it.
- Do not convert a short demonstration into a long-term durability claim.
- Use user-owned demonstration photos first and merchant-approved images only for missing views.

When no product was used, write a buying guide rather than a review.

## Fact Labels

Distinguish internally during drafting:

- Observed: supplied by the user from a visit or use.
- Official: supported by a current official source.
- Inference: a conclusion drawn from compared facts.
- Recommendation: editorial guidance with stated conditions.

Do not present an inference or recommendation as an observed fact.

## Stop Conditions

Block publication when:

- First-person experience appears in a `research_only` package.
- Original photos lack an ownership or reuse basis.
- Sponsored content lacks a visible disclosure.
- A current facility, price, schedule, or product condition is inferred from an old photo without a checked date.
- AI imagery could be mistaken for proof of a real place, product test, or event screen.
