# Monetization Strategy

Use this before selecting revenue-oriented topics. The goal is not to maximize affiliate links; it is to grow trust, search exposure, and buyer intent together.

For AdSense, SEO income, blog monetization, AI-search, traffic-growth, or monthly-income strategy posts, also read `adsense-seo-growth-playbook.md`. That playbook is the stricter rule when a source text contains income claims, policy deadlines, CPC/CTR/RPM numbers, or YMYL-sensitive topics.

## Revenue Lanes

Use four lanes with different roles.

| Lane | Platform fit | Role | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Naver AdPost | Naver Blog | Baseline traffic income and trust signal | Do not expect high revenue from ads alone. |
| Coupang Partners | Naver Blog, Tistory | Main conversion income from buyer-intent posts | Disclose affiliate relationship and avoid link-heavy posts. |
| AdSense/AdFit | Tistory | Supplemental display ad income from searchable evergreen posts | Keep content useful without forcing ad clicks. |
| Sponsored/review work | Later stage | Extra income after the blog has proof of audience | Reject off-niche or low-trust offers. |

## Daily Mix

For a three-draft day outside recovery mode, prefer:

- 1 current information/news/benefit post.
- 1 seasonal search-demand post.
- 1 revenue post, usually a Coupang Partners TOP 5 or buying guide.

Weekly mix target:

- Information and benefit posts: 45% to 55%.
- Seasonal problem-solving posts: 20% to 30%.
- Affiliate or direct revenue posts: 20% to 30%.
- Sponsored or explicit promotional posts: 0% until the blog has stable trust and search inflow.

If the blog shows weak exposure, reduce affiliate posts first and publish stronger information posts for one week.

In traffic recovery mode, publish no more than two affiliate-first posts per week. Tie them to proven family-outing, camping, seasonal-life, or model-specific inflow rather than unrelated high-value categories.

## Revenue Topic Score

Score each revenue topic from 1 to 5.

| Factor | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Timeliness | Is the topic relevant now because of season, event, policy, holiday, or news? |
| Buyer intent | Would a reader naturally compare or buy after reading? |
| Order value | Is the likely basket size meaningful enough to justify the post? |
| Search demand | Are people likely searching this exact problem or product category? |
| Trust fit | Can the post help readers without exaggerated claims or fake experience? |
| Original value | Can the post add tables, checklists, price bands, cautions, or decision criteria? |
| Low-quality safety | Is it distinct from recent posts and not link-stuffed, duplicated, or thin? |

Decision:

- 30 to 35: strong revenue candidate.
- 25 to 29: usable if current sources and angle are strong.
- 20 to 24: keep as support content or rewrite the angle.
- Below 20: reject.

Reject immediately if trust fit or low-quality safety is below 3.

## Conversion Structure

Use this structure for buyer-intent posts:

1. Reader situation: explain who needs this now.
2. Selection criteria: price band, use case, capacity/spec, brand/seller caution, return-risk.
3. Comparison table: show why options differ.
4. Individual recommendations: include who it fits and who should skip it.
5. Practical buying tip: timing, bundle, installation, warranty, size, compatibility, or event benefit.
6. Disclosure and source notes: include checked date for price/event/ranking-sensitive information.

Do not claim exact sales volume, "best-selling", "lowest price", or guaranteed savings unless verified from a current reliable source.

## Revenue Strategy Explainers

Use this for information posts about AdSense, AdPost, SEO income, blogging, platform policy, or side-income strategy when no product link is central.

Prefer this structure:

1. Common claim and reality check: say the goal can be possible only under conditions.
2. First-screen checklist: traffic, search intent, trust, policy risk, and reader value.
3. Trust-first section: explain why high-value topics need sources, cautions, and no fake experience.
4. SEO and UX section: connect search intent, mobile readability, tables, and source dates.
5. Revenue structure section: explain income lanes without forcing clicks or guarantees.
6. Policy risk section: mention ad-click inducement, excessive ad placement, and consent rules only when relevant.
7. Operating order: give a practical sequence the reader can follow.
8. Avoid-list and one next editorial action.

Avoid:

- guaranteed monthly income,
- unsupported CPC averages,
- unverified policy deadlines or revenue-drop percentages,
- screenshots of private dashboards,
- luxury or jackpot visuals,
- AI-generated personal earnings claims.

## Topic Cluster Method

Build revenue from clusters, not isolated product posts.

Example cluster:

- Information post: "Samsung event benefit and date summary."
- Decision post: "What to check before buying during the event."
- Revenue post: "Best-value products to compare during the event."
- Follow-up post: "Reader FAQ and mistakes to avoid."

The first two posts build trust. The revenue post should link back to them naturally.

## Risk Gates

Before draft-save, confirm:

- The post has a clear non-affiliate value even if all links are removed.
- The affiliate disclosure is visible near the top.
- Each recommended item has at least one watch-out.
- The title does not promise impossible savings, guaranteed ranking, or hidden benefits.
- The body does not repeat the same purchase keyword unnaturally.
- The topic does not duplicate a recent post's reader intent.
- Source-sensitive facts include the checked date.

## Performance Loop

After publication, classify each post:

- High views + low clicks: improve CTA, table, and product fit.
- Low views + high clicks: keep the category; improve title and search angle.
- Clicks + no conversion: improve price bands, reader-type picks, and watch-outs.
- Conversions + low risk: repeat the category with a different reader intent.
- Weak exposure after affiliate-heavy week: pause revenue posts and publish trust-building information posts.
