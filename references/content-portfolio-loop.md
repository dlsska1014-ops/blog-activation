# Content Portfolio Loop

Use this to turn daily posts into a connected, measurable content portfolio.

## Before Drafting

1. List the closest existing posts from the last 30 days and durable canonical posts outside that window.
2. Write the exact reader question for each candidate.
3. Decide `new_post` or `update_existing` before writing.
4. Assign a cluster role and identify the intended internal-link path.
5. Reject three-post batches that repeat the same question, product set, or conclusion.

## Link Architecture

- Pillar: broad decision guide; link to distinct event, FAQ, buying, or experience support posts.
- Support: solve one narrow problem; link back to the most relevant pillar.
- Affiliate: answer a real buying question; link to a non-affiliate guide when useful.
- Update: preserve the canonical URL when possible and record what changed.

Use one to three links only when they help the reader's next decision. Avoid repeated exact-match anchor text and link dumping at the end.

## Controlled Learning

For a published post, record comparable snapshots at approximately:

- 24 hours: early exposure and obvious transfer defects,
- 72 hours: initial search-inflow questions and click behavior,
- 7 days: stable topic, title, visual, and conversion signal.

Change one major variable at a time. Do not change both title and thumbnail in the same experiment unless repairing a public defect. Require a repeated signal across at least three comparable posts before updating durable style memory.

## Decision Rules

- Exposure weak, click response acceptable: improve topic/source alignment and internal links before rewriting everything.
- Exposure acceptable, clicks weak: test title or thumbnail, one at a time.
- Reading response weak: improve opening specificity, section order, and visual proof.
- Affiliate clicks weak: improve selection criteria and link placement; do not add more links by default.
- Duplicate intent detected: update or consolidate the canonical post instead of creating another URL.

Never infer missing metrics. Record `unknown` and schedule the next check.
