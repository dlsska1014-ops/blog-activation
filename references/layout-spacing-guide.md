# Layout And Spacing Guide

Use this guide for Naver Blog draft formatting. The goal is comfortable mobile reading with intentional spacing, not decorative emptiness.

## First Screen

The first screen should contain:

1. A situational opening.
2. Why the topic matters now.
3. What the reader will get from the post.
4. One caution or key condition if relevant.

Avoid starting with a long definition or generic "오늘은..." opening.

## Paragraph Spacing

For Naver Blog:

- Use 1 to 3 lines per paragraph.
- Leave one blank line between thought blocks.
- Use two blank lines before a major section when the post feels dense.
- Put warnings, conditions, and final recommendations on their own lines.
- Avoid making every sentence a separate paragraph.

For Tistory:

- Use clearer H2/H3 headings.
- Use fewer blank lines than Naver.
- Keep tables and source notes visible.

## Section Rhythm

Use a rhythm like:

1. Short opening.
2. Quick summary or checklist.
3. Explanation.
4. Table or comparison.
5. Example or budget scenario.
6. FAQ.
7. Practical close.

## Visual Breaks

Add image or table breaks when:

- The reader needs to compare options.
- A benefit calculation is involved.
- There are many conditions or exclusions.
- A product list becomes hard to scan.

Do not add a visual break just to fill space.

## Mobile Scan Check

Before finalizing, check:

- Can the reader understand the point by scanning headings and tables?
- Are dense sections broken into smaller blocks?
- Is the first image or summary card placed early enough?
- Are affiliate disclosures visible before recommendations?
