# Affiliate Scoring

Use this scoring model before drafting a Coupang Partners TOP 5 post. The goal is to choose products that match reader intent and preserve trust, not to chase unsupported claims.

## Product Category Score

Score each candidate category from 1 to 5.

| Factor | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Seasonal urgency | Is demand naturally rising now? |
| Buyer intent | Would a reader be ready to buy after reading? |
| Order value | Is the typical basket value meaningful? |
| Problem clarity | Does the product solve an obvious problem? |
| Comparison value | Can the post help readers choose between options? |
| Repeatability | Can the topic become a recurring cluster? |
| Trust safety | Can it be recommended without exaggerated claims? |

Priority:

- 30 to 35: strong TOP 5 candidate.
- 24 to 29: usable if sources are current and angle is clear.
- Below 24: avoid unless it supports a larger information post.

## Product-Level Score

For each product in the TOP 5, score:

- Price-value fit.
- Review quality signal.
- Brand or seller reliability signal.
- Delivery/availability signal.
- Use-case clarity.
- Gift or household utility.
- Return-risk or disappointment risk.

Do not include products that cannot be explained with a clear use case.

## High-Value Seasonal Clusters

Use these as recurring research prompts, not fixed recommendations:

- Summer: dehumidifiers, air circulators, portable fans, cooling bedding, sunscreen, travel organizers.
- Rainy season: shoe dryers, umbrellas, waterproof bags, dehumidifying goods.
- Moving season: cleaning tools, storage boxes, small appliances, routers, curtains.
- Back-to-school: laptops, tablets, printers, desks, chairs, stationery bundles.
- Parents/gifting: massage devices, health-support appliances, kitchen appliances, bedding.
- Winter: heaters, humidifiers, thermal wear, electric blankets, hand warmers.
- Year-end: gifts, party supplies, calendars, planners, home organization.

## TOP 5 Selection Rules

- Include different reader situations, not five nearly identical items.
- Mix price bands when possible: budget, standard, premium.
- Name watch-outs for every item.
- Avoid medical, financial, or safety claims unless verified and written carefully.
- Keep affiliate disclosure near the top.

## Rejection Reasons

Reject a product if:

- The claim depends on unverified sales volume.
- The use case is unclear.
- Reviews suggest frequent defects or mismatch.
- The price is too volatile to discuss without current checking.
- The product creates legal, medical, or safety risk.
